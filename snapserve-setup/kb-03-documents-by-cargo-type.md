# Documents required by cargo type

Use this to answer "what documents do I need" questions. Always confirm the cargo type first, then list mandatory documents, then mention conditional ones only if relevant.

## General dry cargo
Examples: Machinery parts, Granite tiles, Furniture, Packaged consumer goods.
- Commercial invoice (mandatory)
- Packing list (mandatory)
- Bill of lading (mandatory)
- Shipping bill (export declaration, via ICEGATE) (mandatory)
- Certificate of origin (conditional -- Only if claiming preferential tariff under an FTA)

## Textiles & garments
Examples: Ready-made garments, Fabric rolls, Home textiles.
- Commercial invoice (mandatory)
- Packing list (mandatory)
- Bill of lading (mandatory)
- Certificate of origin (mandatory -- Required for GSP/FTA duty benefit at most destinations)
- Textile testing certificate (conditional -- Some destinations require azo-dye / flammability testing)

## Perishable & food cargo
Examples: Frozen seafood, Dairy, Spices, Processed foods.
- Commercial invoice (mandatory)
- Packing list (mandatory)
- Bill of lading (mandatory)
- Phytosanitary certificate (mandatory -- Issued by Plant Quarantine (for plant-origin goods))
- Health certificate (mandatory -- For animal-origin / marine products)
- Fumigation certificate (mandatory -- Required if wood packaging is used)
- FSSAI export clearance (mandatory)
- Packaging requirement: ISPM-15 compliant wood packaging (fumigated/heat-treated) or non-wood pallets.
- Special handling: Reefer container with continuous temperature logging; pre-cooling before loading.

## Hazardous / dangerous goods
Examples: Industrial chemicals, Lithium batteries, Paints and solvents.
- Commercial invoice (mandatory)
- Packing list (mandatory)
- Bill of lading (mandatory)
- IMO Dangerous Goods Declaration (mandatory)
- Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) (mandatory)
- UN packaging certification (mandatory)
- Carrier DG acceptance approval (mandatory -- Must be obtained before space is booked)
- Packaging requirement: UN-certified packaging matching the IMDG class and packing group.
- Special handling: Requires carrier DG approval before booking; cannot be consolidated with incompatible DG classes.

## Electronics & appliances
Examples: Consumer electronics, Industrial equipment, Battery-powered devices.
- Commercial invoice (mandatory)
- Packing list (mandatory)
- Bill of lading (mandatory)
- BIS / compliance certificate (conditional -- Depends on destination product-safety regime)
- Lithium battery DG documentation (conditional -- Required only if the shipment contains lithium batteries)

## Agricultural bulk & grain
Examples: Rice, Wheat, Pulses, Oilseeds.
- Commercial invoice (mandatory)
- Packing list (mandatory)
- Bill of lading (mandatory)
- Phytosanitary certificate (mandatory)
- Fumigation certificate (mandatory)
- FSSAI export clearance (mandatory)
- Packaging requirement: Food-grade bags; non-wood pallets or ISPM-15 treated wood.

## How to answer a documents question on a call
1. Ask what the cargo is if not already known from the shipment record.
2. Match it to the closest category above.
3. State the mandatory documents first, clearly, one at a time if the customer is writing them down.
4. Mention conditional documents only if they apply to the customer's specific destination or goods.
5. If a document is confirmed missing on the shipment record, tie it to the relevant free-time/demurrage deadline from the destination regulations reference before ending the call.
